Distro Box Wiring

How the blue NAC3MPA-1 power-in feeds the three grey NAC3MPB-1 power-outs inside the printed 1-in 3-out housing. The outputs are simply in parallel on the input: every L together, every N together, every PE together.

Making the connections

The tabs are 3/16" (4.8 × 0.5 mm) FASTON blades, and the plastic ribs around each tab run past its tip, so the receptacle has to leave the tab straight back before the wire turns. Use fully insulated straight 4.8 mm receptacles sized for 2.5 mm² (blue barrel), crimped with a ratchet tool. Two ways to build each bus:

  • Star splice. One lead from the input tab to a 4-way lever connector (Wago 221-415 or equal), then one lead from it to each output tab. Twelve receptacles, three lever connectors, no doubled crimps. Easiest to inspect and to change later.
  • Daisy chain. Input tab to OUT 1, OUT 1 to OUT 2, OUT 2 to OUT 3, with two conductors crimped into the receptacle at each intermediate tab. Only sound with a receptacle whose barrel is rated for two 2.5 mm² conductors; a standard blue barrel is not.

Before closing the lid

  1. Tug every crimp. A receptacle that moves on its tab or a wire that moves in its barrel gets redone.
  2. Continuity, input to each output: L to L, N to N, PE to PE on all three. Every reading near zero ohms.
  3. Isolation: L to N, L to PE and N to PE all open at the input with nothing plugged in.
  4. Dress the L and PE wires in one side channel and N in the other, clear of the lid-screw bosses, then fit the lid.

powerCON is keyed, so L and N cannot be swapped at a plug; they can only be swapped by wiring the wrong tabs, which the continuity check catches. Neutrik rates the system as a connector without breaking capacity: plug and unplug with the load off. A printed PLA or PETG box is not a certified enclosure; treat this as a bench or personal-use build.
